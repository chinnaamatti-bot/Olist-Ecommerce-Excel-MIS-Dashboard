## 📌 Executive Summary
An end-to-end **Management Information System (MIS) Dashboard** engineered in **Microsoft Excel** using **Power Query, Advanced Formulas, and Dynamic Pivot Modeling** across **1,18,434** merged e-commerce transactions (2016–2018).

## 🛠️ Technical Workflow (Power Query → Formulas → Dashboard)

### 1. Power Query Layer (Data Ingestion & ETL)
* **Relational Joins:** Merged three separate Olist tables (`Orders`, `Payments`, and `Items`) on `order_id` using Power Query Merge Queries.
* **Time-Intelligence Extraction:** Extracted `Hour`, `Month Name`, and `Year` directly from raw purchase timestamps.
* **Data Type Standardization:** Standardized mixed text fields into strict Date and Currency formats.

### 2. Advanced Formulas & MIS Audit Tab
![MIS Formulas Audit](mis_formulas_preview.png)
* **Order Segmentation (`IFS`):** Classified transactions into **Tier 1 VIP (≥500)**, **Tier 2 (200–499)**, and **Tier 3 (<200)**.
* **Delivery & Freight Tracking (`IFERROR`):** Built error-safe lead-time and freight share columns (`Actual_Delivery_Days`, `Estimated_vs_Actual_Variance`, and `Freight_Share_Pct`).
* **Live MIS Audit Sheet:** Engineered an interactive query tab using `INDEX/MATCH`, `SUMIFS`, `COUNTBLANK`, and dynamic `DATE()` range criteria.

### 3. Pivot Modeling & Interactive Dashboard
* **Backend Analytics:** Built 5 structured Pivot Tables covering Yearly KPIs, Month-on-Month (MoM) Growth %, Order Tiers, Peak Hours, and Payment Shares.
* **Global Cross-Filtering:** Connected **`Order_Tier`** and **`Year`** Slicers across all backend Pivot Tables via **Report Connections**.

## 🎯 20 Business Questions & Project Insights

### 📈 Sales & Revenue Performance
1. **Total revenue and total volume of orders processed?**
   * **Insight:** Generated **₹2,04,70,726.66** in total revenue across **1,18,434** processed orders.
2. **Which Order Tiers drive the highest sales revenue vs. volume?**
   * **Insight:** **Tier 3 (<200)** leads volume with **91,323 orders** (**₹83,50,584.18**), while **Tier 1 VIP (≥500)** drives **₹59,77,008.23** from just **6,206 orders**.
3. **What is the month-on-month (MoM) revenue growth trend?**
   * **Insight:** Strong seasonal surges occur in **November (+44.33%)**, **February (+21.90%)**, and **May (+11.62%)**, with post-peak dips in **September (-51.63%)**.
4. **What is the Average Order Value (AOV) and growth across years?**
   * **Insight:** Revenue grew from **₹76,528.26** (2016, AOV **₹188.96**) to **₹92,66,612.28** (2017, AOV **₹171.27**) and **₹1,11,27,586.12** (2018, AOV **₹174.08**).
5. **Which year recorded the highest order traffic?**
   * **Insight:** **2018** captured the highest volume with **63,924 orders** (**53.97%** of all historical transactions).

### 🛠️ Advanced Formulas & Data Auditing
6. **How are high-value VIP orders flagged dynamically?**
   * **Insight:** Segmented automatically via `IFS` into **Tier 1 VIP (5.24%)**, **Tier 2 (17.65%)**, and **Tier 3 (77.11%)**.
7. **How can an interviewer look up any Order ID's payment method live?**
   * **Insight:** Solved on `MIS Formulas` using `=IFERROR(INDEX(..., MATCH(B1, ..., 0)), "Not Found")` for instant record lookup.
8. **How is actual delivery time calculated without breaking on blank dates?**
   * **Insight:** Wrapped date subtractions inside `IFERROR(..., "")` so unshipped orders remain clean blanks rather than `#VALUE!` errors.
9. **What is the exact revenue generated by credit cards?**
   * **Insight:** Isolated error-free via `SUMIFS`, confirming **Credit Cards** generate **76.67%** of total platform revenue.
10. **How many orders have missing delivery dates?**
    * **Insight:** Audited using `COUNTBLANK` on `order_delivered_customer_date` to flag pending or canceled shipments.

### 🚚 Logistics & Supply Chain Analysis
11. **How does actual delivery time compare against estimated delivery dates?**
    * **Insight:** Tracked via `Estimated_vs_Actual_Variance` (`delivered_date - estimated_date`) to separate early deliveries from SLA breaches.
12. **How can management isolate delayed shipments from on-time deliveries?**
    * **Insight:** Filtering `Estimated_vs_Actual_Variance > 0` isolates late shipments requiring carrier intervention.
13. **What share of total order cost goes toward freight?**
    * **Insight:** Calculated per row using `=IFERROR(freight_value / (price + freight_value), 0)` to identify shipping cost sensitivity on low-ticket items.
14. **How does order value tier compare against fulfillment load?**
    * **Insight:** Visualized via a dual-axis **Combo Chart** showing **Tier 1 VIP** delivers **29.20%** of revenue from just **5.24%** of shipments.
15. **What is the peak order hour window for logistics staffing?**
    * **Insight:** Order volume climbs from **10:00 AM (7,392 orders)** and peaks between **1:00 PM and 4:00 PM** (**7,729 to 7,944 orders/hour**).

### 💳 Payment Operations & Installment Risk
16. **What is the preferred payment method layout across the user base?**
    * **Insight:** **Credit Card** dominates at **76.67%**, followed by **Boleto (19.96%)**, **Voucher (2.11%)**, and **Debit Card (1.25%)**.
17. **How does payment behavior differ across high-ticket vs. low-ticket tiers?**
    * **Insight:** Cross-filtering via the `Order_Tier` Slicer shows **Tier 1 VIP** orders rely heavily on credit cards, while **Tier 3** shows higher Boleto and Voucher usage.
18. **Which payment method carries the highest overall revenue contribution?**
    * **Insight:** **Credit Cards** generate nearly **4x** the revenue of Boleto (**76.67% vs. 19.96%**).
19. **What percentage of revenue is tied up in long-term (>6) installments?**
    * **Insight:** Calculated on `MIS Formulas` via `SUMIFS(..., payment_installments, ">6") / SUM(...)` to monitor working-capital exposure.
20. **How can management filter all KPIs dynamically by Year and Order Tier?**
    * **Insight:** Enabled via global **Slicers** (`Year` and `Order_Tier`) linked across all 5 Pivot Tables, alongside dynamic `DATE()` range `SUMIFS` on `MIS Formulas`.
